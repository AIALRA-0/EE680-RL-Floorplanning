    How should these terms be understood
    

    Candidate positions and action masks
    A candidate proposes a lower-left coordinate (x,y) for the current macro. Mask entry i describes whether it may execute, with i=y*width+x. Overlap or boundary failure makes it false; every required check must pass for true.
    

    Environment and policy responsibilities
    The environment enforces physical constraints and returns feedback. Chapter 01 validates it using the first legal position. Chapters 02-04 use an actor/critic, recorded samples and real PPO updates. Their trajectories are stored separately.
    

    Why is touching allowed while overlap is rejected
    Positive-area overlap requires positive-length intersections in both x and y. If B1 starts at x=25 while B0 ends at 25, the strict comparison 25<25 is false. Their edges touch without overlap.
    

    Why is intermediate HPWL only partial
    A net may still have unplaced endpoints. The environment computes spans using currently placed endpoints; complete circuit HPWL is available after placement finishes. Endpoints use lower-left corners in both shared scoring and the received SA-SP implementation.
    

    How does this connect to PPO training
    The actor produces action probabilities, masks illegal choices and samples a legal action. The critic estimates future return. Actual feedback is retained in a rollout, used to compute advantages and update parameters, and the updated policy supplies later decisions.

      How can you test your understanding without notes
      First explain state -> encoding -> actor/critic -> mask -> action -> env.step -> reward -> rollout -> GAE/return -> PPO loss -> backward -> Adam -> changed probabilities. Answer each question before opening its explanation.
      How are unplaced zero coordinates distinguished from placement at the origin
      The placed flag differs; current identifies the macro receiving a decision. Coordinates alone do not distinguish these cases.
      What coordinate corresponds to i=W
      x=i%W=0 and y=i//W=1: the first anchor of the next row. Geometry checks still determine whether the rectangle fits.
      Why stop instead of sampling when every action is illegal
      There is no executable action. The environment records failure, returns -2 and terminates. masked_distribution also rejects an all-false mask.
      At termination, with reward=-2 and V=-0.5, what is delta
      Hand example: terminal bootstrap is zero, so delta=-2-(-0.5)=-1.5. Carry across the episode boundary is zero and return=-2.
      With old p=0.2, new p=0.3, A=2 and epsilon=0.2, what is policy loss
      Hand example: ratio=1.5, objectives are 3 and 2.4. Negating their minimum gives -2.4. With A=-2, the objectives are -3 and -2.4 and the loss is 3.
      Have parameter values changed immediately after backward
      backward calculates gradients. optimizer.step applies parameter changes after gradient clipping. Gradient clipping differs from clipping PPO's probability-ratio objective.
      Why can sampled evaluation fail after one successful argmax layout
      Different action-selection procedures create different trajectories. The current mask enforces single-step legality, while later macros can still be blocked. Deterministic and repeated sampled completion are therefore recorded separately.
      How do initialize-from and resume differ when scaling inputs
      initialize-from reuses weights with a fresh optimizer and update count. resume restores optimizer, RNG and progress for continuation with the same canvas and reward convention.
